Welcome to my website!

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at [The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)](https://hkust.edu.hk/). **I am on the 2026–2027 economics job market.** You can find my [CV](/cv/) here, and I would be delighted to hear from you at [jhuangde@connect.ust.hk](mailto:jhuangde@connect.ust.hk).

My research lies in **Experimental Economics, Behavioral Economics, and Game Theory**, combining theoretical models with laboratory experiments to study decision-making and strategic interactions.

I am fortunate to be advised by my thesis committee: Prof. [Wooyoung Lim](https://wooyoung78lim.wixsite.com/wooyounglim), Prof. [Yeon-Koo Che](https://www.yeonkooche.com/), Prof. [Rui Tang](https://ruitangeconomics.weebly.com/), and Prof. [Songfa Zhong](https://zhongsongfa.weebly.com/).

Prior to my Ph.D., I received my M.Phil. from HKUST, M.Sc. from The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, and B.A. from Renmin University of China. I have also been a visiting scholar at the [California Institute of Technology](https://www.caltech.edu/) and [Purdue University](https://www.purdue.edu/).
